## 2026-09-18 - Actionable Empty States in CLI Workflows
**Learning:** CLI empty states in terminal workflows are easily mistaken for missing functionality or quiet failures unless accompanied by explicit success indicators or actionable next steps.
**Action:** When a CLI command returns empty results or zero actions to perform, provide actionable tips (e.g., suggesting the next subcommand to run) or clean success indicators (e.g., 'Watchlist is fully up-to-date!').
